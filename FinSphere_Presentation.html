FinSphere AI

The Intelligent Financial Brain for the Modern Consumer

Event: LT Techfest 2026
Team: Meridian

Het Patel  •  Dharmesh Vekaria  •  Aayush Mistry

The Broken Financial Mirror

  • Fragmented Data: Our money is scattered across banks, credit cards, and subscriptions.
  • Reactive, Not Proactive: Budgeting apps only tell you what you already spent.
  • Manual Goal Setting: Planning for goals relies on stressful, static spreadsheets.
  • Lack of Foresight: Traditional tools cannot predict the impact of today’s spending.

Enter FinSphere AI

  • Unified Dashboard: Aggregates all financial data into one seamless view.
  • Proactive Orchestration: Moves from tracking past expenses to prescribing future actions.
  • Advanced Insights: Leverages AI/ML to detect spending anomalies and predict future cash flows.
  • The Six Core Engines: Modular architecture for comprehensive wealth management.

Engineered for Scale & Speed

  • Frontend Layer: Next.js (React 19), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion.
  • Backend Layer: FastAPI, Python, Uvicorn for asynchronous high-performance endpoints.
  • Database: SQLAlchemy and SQLite (scalable relational data modeling).
  • Security & UX: Local-first privacy mode, seamless API integrations.

The Foundation: Tracking with Intelligence

  • Income Engine: Automatically detects recurring salaries vs. one-off freelance income.
  • Expense Engine: Deep categorization of daily outflows.
  • Anomaly Detection: Flags unusual spending spikes or lifestyle inflation immediately.
  • Actionable Advice: Identifies exactly where to cut back to maximize savings.

The Ultimate Defense Against Late Fees

  • Automated Tracking: Detects recurring subscriptions and utility bills.
  • Chronological Timeline: Visualizes exactly when money will leave your account.
  • Smart Matching: Automatically reconciles bank transactions against expected bills.
  • Peace of Mind: Eliminates late fees and subscription traps.

Total Visibility into Your Liquidity

  • Balance Engine: Aggregates checking, savings, and credit to show True Net Available Cash.
  • Pending Reconciliations: Adjusts balances instantly based on pending transactions.
  • Cashflow Engine: Maps the flow of cash in and out over time (burn rate).
  • Liquidity Foresight: Ensures you are never caught cash-poor before payday.

The Crown Jewel: Predictive Goals

Core USP
  • Simulated Trajectories: Input a target (e.g., $10k for a car) and visualize the exact path.
  • Dynamic Pacing: Calculates exact required monthly contributions based on deadline.
  • 'What-If' Scenarios: Simulate how changing your contribution today impacts your completion date.
  • Behavioral Adjustments: The engine adapts to your actual saving habits in real-time.

Designing Trust Through Aesthetics

  • Privacy Mode: Instantly mask sensitive data (balances, transactions) for public viewing.
  • Fluid Interactions: 60fps micro-animations that make finance feel responsive and alive.
  • Dark Mode Optimization: Reduces eye strain and creates a premium, institutional-grade feel.
  • Cognitive Ease: Complex data is simplified into beautiful, easily digestible widgets.

Built on a Secure Foundation

  • Data Integrity: Strict enforcement of foreign keys and relational schemas in SQLAlchemy.
  • Stateless Authentication: (Future integration) Secure JWT handling for user sessions.
  • Asynchronous Processing: FastAPI ensures that complex ML calculations don't block the UI.
  • Extensible API: Ready to integrate with Open Banking (Plaid) for live bank feeds.

The Roadmap Beyond LT Techfest

  • Phase 1: Live Bank API Integrations (Plaid, Stripe).
  • Phase 2: Generative AI Chatbot (Ask: 'Can I afford to eat out tonight?').
  • Phase 3: Mobile Application Deployment (React Native).
  • Phase 4: Investment & Portfolio Tracking (Stocks, Crypto integrations).
